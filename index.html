Diff is Sim % minus Book % (for example 10% Sim vs 8% Book = +2.0%). Book % is the raw implied probability from the odds, so it includes the sportsbook's margin. Across a full field the book probabilities add up to more than 100%, which pushes Diff slightly negative on average.

How to use the model

The model rates every golfer in the field by combining dozens of stats. You decide how much each stat matters. The ratings, projections (fpts) and salary-value columns update the moment you change anything.

1. Start with the default weights

The page opens with Noto's default weights. The Weight % row under the column headers shows how much each stat counts toward a golfer's rating. A dash means the stat isn't used by default. Click any golfer to see the rank behind each of their stats.

2. Set your own weights

  1. Click ⚙ Weights to open the sliders. Every stat has one, including stats that aren't in the default model.
  2. Drag a slider up to make that stat matter more, or to zero to ignore it. Weights are scaled to add up to 100%, so only the balance between stats matters.
  3. Watch the Rating, fpts and Weight % row change as you go. The sliders for a stat group show that group's total share.
  4. Click Reset to default weights any time to go back to Noto's weights.
Your weights and ADJ entries are saved in this browser, so they will still be here when you come back to the same slate.

3. Add manual adjustments (ADJ)

Have a read the stats can't capture, like an injury note, a course fit or a hot practice round? Type a percent into the ADJ box for that golfer. 10 raises their rating and projection by 10%. -5 lowers them by 5%. Press Enter or click away to apply. Clear ADJ removes all of them.

4. Choose how projections are calculated

Use the fpts menu next to the search box. Rating as-is uses the rating itself as the projection. Scaled to points range stretches the ratings onto a fantasy-point range you set with the Floor (lowest projection) and Top (best projection) boxes. Either way, a higher number means a higher projection.

5. Explore the table

  • Click a column header to sort. Click it again to reverse the order.
  • Type in the search box to find a golfer.
  • Use Stats ▾ to hide or show whole stat groups.
  • Colors run from orange (worst in the field) to teal (best) for ranks, ratings and value.
  • Golfer and rank stay in place as you scroll right. The Weight % row's label does too.

6. Export your projections

  1. Set your weights and adjustments the way you want them.
  2. Click ⬇ Export CSV at the top right. A file named golf_<event>.csv downloads.
  3. The file has three columns, rg_id, name and fpts, sorted from the highest projection to the lowest. Golfers who have withdrawn are exported with 0.
  4. Upload that CSV to the RotoGrinders lineup builder as your projections.
The export uses whatever the table shows right now: your weights, your ADJ entries and your fpts setting.

The Simulations tab

This tab shows the probabilities from Noto's simulations next to the sportsbook odds for each market (Win, Top 10, Top 20). Diff shows where the simulations disagree with the market. Teal means the simulations like the golfer more than the books do, which can point to value. Diff is Sim % minus Book %. This tab doesn't change when you move the weights.

Good to know

  • If a golfer has no data for a stat (for example a golfer new to the PGA Tour), the model estimates that stat from their sportsbook odds and the simulations. Those cells show a hatched dash.
  • A golfer with no history at this course counts as slightly below average for Course History.
  • A golfer marked WD has withdrawn. They get a rating and projection of 0 and are sorted to the bottom.

Glossary

Every stat is shown as a rank among the golfers in this week's field: 1 is the best. The three columns under most stats are time windows: the last 3, 6 and 18 months.

Table columns

Rating
The model's overall score for a golfer. It blends all the stats using your weights, and an average golfer is about 50.
ADJ
Your manual adjustment, in percent. It raises or lowers a golfer's rating and projection.
fpts
The projection: fantasy points based on the rating (see the fpts setting). This is what the CSV export uses.
DK Sal / FD Sal
DraftKings and FanDuel salaries.
DK pOWN / FD pOWN
Projected ownership: the share of lineups expected to use the golfer.
DK / $ and FD / $
Value: rating per $1,000 of salary. Higher means more rating for the price.
Weight %
The share of a rating that comes from each stat with the current weights.
WD
Withdrawn from the event.

Stats

Strokes Gained: Off-the-Tee
Strokes gained from tee shots compared with the field average.
Good Drive %
The share of drives that finish in a good position.
Driving Distance
Average length of drives.
Driving Accuracy
The share of drives that finish in the fairway.
Strokes Gained: Approach
Strokes gained on approach shots into the green.
Weighted Strokes Gained: Approach
A weighted version of approach strokes gained built for this model. It is left out of the table when there isn't data for it.
Strokes Gained: Around-the-Green
Strokes gained on short-game shots near the green, such as chips and bunker shots.
Strokes Gained: Putting
Strokes gained on the greens. The Grass Type column uses only rounds on the same kind of grass as this week's greens.
Expected Hole Performance
How a golfer scores on holes compared with what is expected for those holes.
Birdie or Better %
The share of holes played in a birdie or better.
Bogey Avoidance
How well a golfer avoids bogeys or worse. Higher is better.
Course History
A 0 to 100 rating of how the golfer has played at this course. No history counts as slightly below average.
Comps (Similar Courses)
Ratings of a golfer's results at courses similar in style to this week's. They only show when the model uses them for the event.
Cut %
The share of events where the golfer made the cut.
Top 25%, Top 10%, Top 5%
The share of events where the golfer finished in the top 25, top 10 or top 5.
Form and Field Strength
Form is a golfer's strokes gained per round over the last 3, 8 and 18 months. Field Strength is a 0 to 100 measure of how strong the fields the golfer has played in were. A higher number means tougher recent fields.

Terms

Strokes gained (SG)
How many strokes better or worse than the field average a golfer is in one part of the game.
L3 / L6 / L18 Months
The last 3, 6 or 18 months of results.
Rank
Where a golfer stands among this week's field for a stat. 1 is best.
Z-score
How far above or below the field average a golfer is for a stat, in standard deviations. The model blends z-scores, not raw numbers, so stats on different scales can be combined.
Estimated from odds
When a golfer has no data for a stat, the model fills it in from their simulated and sportsbook probabilities. Those cells show a hatched dash.
Sim %
The probability from Noto's simulations that a golfer reaches that result (win, top 10, top 20).
Book odds / Book %
The sportsbook odds, and the probability they imply. Book % includes the sportsbook's margin.
Diff
Sim % compared with Book %. Teal means the simulations like the golfer more than the market does.
Scaled to points range
An fpts option that stretches ratings onto the Floor to Top range you set.
